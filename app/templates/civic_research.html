01 / Get the source right.

Extract → transform → load

The research question is small: can a resident ask about imported council records, inspect the relevant evidence and receive an answer that stays within it? The corpus comes from official source records displayed by this site. Account forms, navigation and design-preview copy are excluded from retrieval.

Extract

Read the public feed.

The Newark City Clerk links to the city's Legistar agenda service. The importer requests a bounded set of public meeting and agenda metadata from that official API.

An anonymous read succeeded for this source. Legistar's API documentation says token rules vary by client; this observation does not promise permanent access or authorize reuse of unrelated attachments and assets.

Transform

Keep meaning and provenance.

Validate record IDs and dates, normalize text, preserve the local meeting timezone, and retain the original URL, fetch time, source update time and content hash.

Build readable meeting or agenda-item text, then split only when needed. An agenda's publication status does not establish that an item passed, and a listed schedule does not prove a meeting occurred.

Load

Save a consistent version.

Commit sources and their dependent chunks in one SQLite transaction. Unchanged imports reuse stable identities; changed sources replace their old searchable passages together.

Refresh compatible embeddings for changed chunks. Failed or incomplete imports preserve the last successful snapshot. A source change during answer preparation returns a retryable conflict instead of mixing revisions.

Executed import: the November 10 research snapshot contains five recent meeting records and two selected agenda items: seven sources and eight chunks. After source-link repairs, a repeat import reported all seven unchanged. These are observed bounded-import results, not a complete Newark calendar.

02 / Make useful pieces.

A chunk is a passage with its context attached.

Current method

Keep a short civic record together.

One short meeting record becomes one passage containing its date, scheduled time, place and source. A longer agenda item is split at paragraph or word boundaries while retaining its heading and source version.

The present cap is 1,600 Unicode characters, with zero overlap. Characters are not model tokens; this boundary experiment does not establish an optimal tokenizer setting.

For the November 5, 2026 record, preserving 12:30 PM, America/New_York, Council Chamber together makes its schedule easier to inspect. The date must remain attached so a past record does not appear to describe an upcoming meeting.

The comparison we actually ran

Paragraphs versus fixed windows.

Same inputs and size cap

Eight chunks. The full date/time/location span stayed together in all five meeting records.

Eight chunks. The same span stayed together in all five meeting records.
What this establishes
No advantage was observed on that boundary check. Short records made both methods sufficient for the tested span. Broader retrieval and answer-quality experiments remain open.

03 / Search, then answer.

Different rankings answer different questions.
Lexical baseline

FTS5 + BM25

SQLite FTS5 searches the imported text. BM25 ranks word matches using factors such as term frequency and document length. This is useful for dates, ordinance numbers and exact names.

Exact words can still miss a differently phrased question. Retrieval is evaluated separately from the generated answer.

Semantic retrieval

Embeddings + cosine distance

OpenAI's text-embedding-3-small represents each passage and question as a numeric vector. The evaluated index uses 1,536 dimensions. sqlite-vec retrieves nearby vectors using cosine distance.

Nearby text can be irrelevant or incomplete. Similarity does not establish truth, correct dates or support for every answer claim.

Bounded combination

Reciprocal rank fusion

The hybrid path combines lexical and vector rankings by adding 1 / (60 + rank) for each appearance, then selects up to five passages.

RRF combines ranks without treating BM25 scores and vector distances as the same scale. Our small evaluation did not show a source-coverage advantage over keyword search.

Generation

An answer must point back to evidence.

The bounded retrieved passages are supplied to gpt-5-mini. The model is instructed to use that evidence, preserve dates and uncertainty, ignore instructions within source text, and report insufficient evidence when necessary.

The application accepts only references to retrieved current chunk IDs and adds the source links itself. Citation validity is a useful mechanical check; claim-level support still needs inspection.

Storage choice

A small, separate civic database.

Source records
Official IDs, title, text, dates, URLs, versions, hashes and link-check provenance.
Chunks and vectors
Passage text, heading, parent source/version, embedding model and compatible vector configuration.
Private account data
Accounts and sessions use separate storage. They are not a RAG corpus. API keys stay server-side, and questions are not saved by the application.
Graph alternative
AgendaItem → LISTED_AT → Meeting is a useful relationship sketch. Native graph storage and automatic extraction are deferred until a bounded test shows a missing capability. A listing does not imply approval.

04 / What informed the decisions.

Five primary research papers

These papers inform design choices. Their reported benchmark results do not become results for Newark Civic Pulse.

  1. Lewis et al. (2020) · Retrieval-Augmented Generation

    Finding used: external retrieved passages can supply evidence to a generator. Decision: retrieve current civic chunks before generating an answer and retain the trace. Limit: this API-based application does not reproduce the paper's trained retriever–generator system. Sections 2–2.2 informed the review.

  2. Karpukhin et al. (2020) · Dense Passage Retrieval

    Finding used: dense passage/query representations support retrieval, which can be assessed separately from generation. Decision: compare required-source coverage at a fixed top-five budget against the lexical baseline. Limit: its benchmark gains do not establish gains for our corpus or OpenAI embeddings. Sections 3 and 5 informed the review.

  3. Wang et al. (2024) · RAG pipeline and chunking choices

    Finding used: chunk boundaries, metadata and retrieval combinations introduce quality/efficiency tradeoffs. Decision: retain meaningful civic-record boundaries and compare paragraph splitting with fixed windows. Limit: the paper's models, sizes and preferred pipeline require local evidence before adoption. Sections 3.2, 3.4 and 5.1 informed the review.

  4. Es et al. (2023) · RAGAS

    Finding used: faithfulness, answer relevance and context relevance are different evaluation dimensions. Decision: review retrieved evidence separately from whether the answer's claims follow from it. Limit: no RAGAS score or automated-judge result is claimed here. Section 3 informed the review.

  5. Liu et al. (2023) · Lost in the Middle

    Finding used: evidence placement and distracting long context affected the models/tasks studied. Decision: supply a small ranked context rather than the whole site. Limit: a position effect has not been measured for this application's current model. Section 2.3 informed the review.

The professor's reference

Useful contracts, with an explicit implementation boundary.

The reviewed is373-rag revision 242fd57 provides documentation, synthetic labs and a static scaffold. Its status record identifies the research backend as planned.

We adopted the ideas of versioned evidence, dependent-artifact readiness and bounded retrieval. Its receipts do not establish this project's results. The current implementation uses a direct Python importer; Rust/NATS remain a later experiment, and native graph publication is deferred.

06 / Keep the limits visible.

Evidence supports a bounded claim.
What the corpus can establish

Listed facts, with dates.

Imported meeting dates, scheduled times, locations and selected agenda listings can support limited questions. The collection cannot establish the next city meeting, a total budget, a vote or final passage without the relevant evidence.

What remains to test

A broader, human-reviewed sample.

More source families, questions, tokenizer-aware boundaries, citation support and useful peer feedback need checking. Dollar spend and latency were not measured in the frozen evaluation. A small success is not a general accuracy or security certification.
